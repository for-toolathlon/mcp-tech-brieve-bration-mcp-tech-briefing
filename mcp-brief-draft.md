# MCP tech brief - draft v0

## Claim 1 - Definition
The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open-source standard for connecting AI applications to external systems such as data sources and tools, as described in the official documentation at modelcontextprotocol.io.

## Claim 2 - Origin and adoption
MCP was created by Anthropic and announced in November 2024, and in March 2025 both OpenAI and Google announced they would adopt the protocol.

## Claim 3 - Office locations
Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google are all headquartered in San Francisco, California.
